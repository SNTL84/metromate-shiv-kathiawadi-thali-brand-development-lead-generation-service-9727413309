## 🏛️ What Is This Project?

**Shiv Kathiyawadi Thali** is a dine-in thali restaurant in **Palanpur, Surat**, run by **Munjalbhai Mehta**. Like most neighbourhood thali joints, the business ran entirely on foot traffic and word of mouth — no digital presence, no way for a customer to check the menu, call, or place an order without physically walking in.

This repository documents the **MetroMate Performance Marketing Services** engagement that gave the restaurant its first digital front door: a promotional landing page with direct **WhatsApp and call integration**, so a customer scrolling Instagram or Google can go from *"never heard of this place"* to *"message sent"* in one tap.

## ❓ Q&A

**Q: Why did a restaurant with no website need a landing page instead of a full site?**
A: For a single-location thali restaurant, the goal isn't SEO or a multi-page site — it's removing friction between "someone sees the name" and "someone messages to order." A single, fast-loading page with WhatsApp and Call built in does that job without the overhead of a full build.

**Q: Why WhatsApp and Call specifically, not a contact form?**
A: In Surat's F&B market, customers overwhelmingly prefer WhatsApp or a direct call over filling a form and waiting for a reply. Meeting customers on the channel they already use converts better than making them adopt a new one.

**Q: Is this a one-off, or part of a repeatable service?**
A: It's part of the same **MetroMate Performance Marketing Services** B2C playbook used across other Surat F&B clients (see the Mahadev Restaurant case study) — landing page + WhatsApp/Call integration is a repeatable, fast-turnaround package for local food businesses.

**Q: What would the next phase look like?**
A: Typical next steps for this kind of client are a Google Business Profile push, a lightweight digital menu (photos + pricing), and adding the same lead-capture pattern used in MetroMate's other restaurant work (see [`recent-work`](https://github.com/SNTL84/Metromate-Website-Roadmap) for the Mahadev Excel menu system and WhatsApp order-sheet approach as a reference).

**Q: Where does this fit in the SNTL84 / MetroMate structure?**
A: This is a **MetroMate Performance Marketing Services** delivery — the same B2C service line responsible for the Mahadev Restaurant flagship case study inside the [Metromate-Website-Roadmap](https://github.com/SNTL84/Metromate-Website-Roadmap) repo.
